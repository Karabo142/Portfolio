During the initial UI/UX phase, our primary risk was avoiding feature creep and narrowing down extensive marketplace research into clear, actionable design choices.Using daily standups allowed us to identify early blockers—such as determining the exact number of screens to include in the user walkthrough without cluttering the presentation. By discussing this daily, we established clear limits and prioritized core user journeys over redundant edge-case screens.

Tracking progress daily enabled us to break down complex business requirements into distinct visual deliverables. Iteratively revising our graphics ensured that pricing tiers remained clear, transparent, and easy to evaluate at a glance.
